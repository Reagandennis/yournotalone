Not Alone is a mental wellness app for anxiety, stress, low mood and sleep: a daily mood tracker, private journal, guided meditations, breathing exercises and calming sounds, with a real, licensed therapist a tap away when you want more than an app. Your first therapy session is free.

The name comes from the Greek "Δεν είσαι μόνος", which means "you are not alone". That is the whole idea.

TALK TO A LICENSED THERAPIST
Private one-on-one video sessions with licensed therapists, booked right from the app. Tell us what you want to work on and when you are free, and our care team matches you personally. No subscription needed, no card for your first session.

DAILY MOOD TRACKER
How are you feeling today? Log your mood in one tap and watch small, honest check-ins build into a picture of your wellbeing over weeks and months.

JOURNAL
A private space to write it out: free writing, gratitude, or whatever today calls for. Your entries stay yours.

MEDITATION & BREATHING EXERCISES
Guided meditations for anxiety relief, morning grounding, body scan and sleep wind-down, plus breathing exercises (box breathing, 4-7-8 calm, slow exhale) with a calm visual to breathe along with. A few minutes is enough.

SLEEP & FOCUS SOUNDS
Rainfall, ocean waves, white noise, deep focus and crickets at night. Loops that stay with you while you focus, drift off or settle down.

RECOVERY TOOLS
Taking things one day at a time? The urge-surfing guide is there for the hardest minutes, free for everyone, always, alongside a daily pledge and trigger log.

GENTLE REMINDERS
Optional daily nudges so looking after yourself becomes a habit, not an afterthought.

FREE & PREMIUM
The essentials are free: mood check-ins, urge surfing, your first therapy session and a taste of everything. Premium unlocks unlimited journaling, the full meditation library, all sounds and progress insights through an auto-renewing subscription you can cancel anytime in Google Play.

A NOTE THAT MATTERS
This app supports your wellbeing. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or emergency care. If you are in crisis or immediate danger, call your local emergency number right away.

You don't have to do this alone.
