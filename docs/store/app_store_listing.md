## Description (4000 max)

yournotalone means exactly that: you're not alone. A gentle mental-wellness companion for looking after your mind a little every day, with a real, licensed therapist behind it when you want more than an app.

DAILY CHECK-INS
How are you feeling today? Log your mood in one tap and watch small, honest check-ins build into a picture of your wellbeing over time.

JOURNAL
A private space to write it out: free writing, gratitude, guided prompts, or whatever today calls for. Your entries stay yours.

MEDITATIONS & BREATHING
Guided sessions like Morning Grounding, Anxiety Relief, Body Scan and Sleep Wind-down, plus breathing exercises (box breathing, 4-7-8, slow exhale) with a calm visual to breathe along with. A few minutes is enough.

TOOLKITS FOR HARD DAYS
Practical exercises for anxiety, low mood, stress and sleep, drawn from cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT): thought records, planning small steps, winding down for bed.

WELLBEING SOUNDS
Rainfall, ocean waves, a distant storm, fireside, white and brown noise, deep focus and crickets at night: loops that stay with you while you focus, drift off or settle down.

RECOVERY TOOLS
Taking things one day at a time? The urge-surfing guide is there for the hardest minutes, free for everyone, always, alongside a daily pledge and trigger log.

TALK TO A REAL THERAPIST
Private one-to-one video sessions with licensed therapists, right in the app. Tell us what you'd like to focus on and when you could meet, and our care team matches you personally. Sessions are US$20 each, paid when you book. Cancel in the app up to 24 hours before for a full refund.

GENTLE REMINDERS
Optional daily nudges so caring for yourself becomes a habit, not an afterthought.

FREE & PREMIUM
The essentials are free, including mood check-ins, urge surfing and a taste of everything. Premium unlocks unlimited journaling, the full meditation and sound library, every toolkit exercise and your progress insights.

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, each starting with a free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, and the subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel any time in your App Store account settings.

A NOTE THAT MATTERS
yournotalone supports your wellbeing, but it is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or emergency care. If you are in crisis or immediate danger, call your local emergency number right away.
