## Short description (80 max)

Mood check-ins, journaling, calming sounds, and a therapist when you need one.

## Full description (4000 max)

Δεν είσαι μόνος is Greek for "you are not alone" — and that's the whole idea.
A gentle mental-wellness companion for looking after your mind a little every
day, with a real, licensed therapist behind it when you want more than an app.

DAILY CHECK-INS
How are you feeling today? Log your mood in one tap and watch honest, tiny
check-ins build into a picture of your wellbeing over time.

JOURNAL & MOOD
A private space to write it out — free writing, gratitude, or whatever today
calls for. Your entries stay yours.

MEDITATIONS & BREATHING
Guided sessions like Morning Grounding, Anxiety Relief, Body Scan and Sleep
Wind-down, plus breathing exercises (Box breathing, 4-7-8 calm, Slow exhale)
with a calm visual to breathe along with. A few minutes is enough.

WELLBEING SOUNDS
Rainfall, ocean waves, white noise, deep focus and crickets at night — loops
that stay with you while you focus, drift off, or settle down.

RECOVERY TOOLS
Taking things one day at a time? The urge-surfing guide is there for the
hardest minutes — free for everyone, always — alongside a daily pledge and
trigger log.

TALK TO A REAL THERAPIST
Private one-on-one sessions with licensed therapists, requested right from
the app. Tell us what you'd like to focus on and when you could meet, and our
care team matches you personally. Sessions are $20 each, paid securely in the
app when you book — no subscription.

GENTLE REMINDERS
Optional daily nudges so caring for yourself becomes a habit, not an
afterthought.

FREE & PREMIUM
The essentials are free, including mood check-ins, urge surfing and a taste
of everything. Premium unlocks unlimited journaling, the full meditation
library, all wellbeing sounds, and progress insights, via an auto-renewing
subscription you can cancel anytime in Google Play.

A NOTE THAT MATTERS
This app supports your wellbeing, but it is not a medical device and does not
provide diagnosis or emergency care. If you are in crisis or immediate
danger, call your local emergency number right away.

You don't have to do this alone.
