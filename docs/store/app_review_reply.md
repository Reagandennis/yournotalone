# App Review reply — Guideline 2.1 Information Needed (Oct 2026)

Thank you for reviewing yournotalone. Answers below; a screen recording from an iPhone running the latest iOS is attached.

1. Screen recording: attached. It starts at launch and shows account creation (with the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy), onboarding, the Premium subscription screen (plan names, lengths, prices, free trials, and links to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy), the main features, crisis resources, account deletion (Settings > Account actions > Delete account), signing up again, and logout/login. Therapy is booked from "Talk to a real therapist" on the Home screen; the demo account below shows a booked request.
User-generated content: journal entries and mood check-ins are private to the user and are never shown to other users. There is no public feed, chat between users, or sharing. The only person-to-person interaction is a scheduled 1:1 video session with a vetted, licensed therapist. After a session the client can rate it and leave a comment that goes to our care team, and users can contact support from Settings at any time.

2. Purpose and audience: yournotalone is a mental-wellness companion for people aged 16 and over who want simple daily tools to look after their mental health: mood check-ins, a private journal, guided meditation and breathing, CBT-based exercises for anxiety, low mood, stress and sleep, calming sounds, and recovery tools (urge surfing, daily pledge). When self-help is not enough, users can book a private video session with a licensed therapist. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or emergency care; crisis resources are shown in the app.

4. External services:
   - Supabase: database, authentication and storage
   - Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In: login
   - RevenueCat with StoreKit: Premium subscriptions (In-App Purchase)
   - Paystack: payment for live 1:1 therapy sessions (guideline 3.1.3(d))
   - LiveKit: real-time video for therapy sessions (not recorded)
   - Resend: transactional emails (booking, receipts, reminders)
   - Sentry: crash and error reporting

5. Regions: the app works the same in all regions. Therapy sessions are priced in US dollars ($20) and charged by Paystack in Kenyan shillings (Ksh 2,600).

6. Regulated services: therapy sessions are delivered by our clinical partner Echo Psychology Group (trading as Echo Health), Nairobi, Kenya. Every therapist is engaged by Echo and holds current registration and a practising licence from the Counsellors and Psychologists Board of Kenya. Echo is responsible for clinical care, records, risk assessment and complaints; session content and clinical notes are held only by Echo and are not shared with us. Echo's partnership confirmation letter, with its practitioner licence register, is attached. All third-party content (sounds, fonts) is used under licence.

7. In-App Purchase: one auto-renewable subscription group, "Premium", with Pro Monthly ($9.99/month) and Pro Annual ($59.99/year), each with a free trial. Premium unlocks unlimited journaling, the full meditation and sound library, all toolkit exercises and progress insights. To reach it: the last step of onboarding, any locked item, or Settings > "Free plan" card > Upgrade. Therapy sessions are live services between two people, so they are paid outside In-App Purchase as permitted by guideline 3.1.3(d).
