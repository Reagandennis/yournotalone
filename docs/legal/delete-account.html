Delete your account

yournotalone ("You're not alone") · Techgetafrica

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time. You do not have to give a reason, and nobody will try to talk you out of it.

From inside the app

This is the fastest way, and it happens immediately:

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Account actions and tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is removed straight away and you are signed out.

By email

If you can no longer sign in, write to reaganprezzo@gmail.com from the email address on the account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deleted permanently, and not recoverable:

  • Your account, name, email address and profile photo
  • Every journal entry
  • Every mood check-in
  • Recovery commitments, daily pledges and streaks
  • Craving and trigger logs
  • Therapy session requests and any notes attached to them
  • Reminder settings stored on your device

Kept, and why: records we are legally required to hold — chiefly purchase and tax records for a subscription, which are held by Google Play or Apple rather than by us, and which we cannot delete on your behalf. Encrypted backups are purged on a rolling 30-day cycle.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a paid subscription, because the subscription belongs to your Google Play or Apple account, not to us. Cancel it in the Play Store or App Store first, or it will keep renewing.
